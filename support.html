Soneto Support

Published: October 2, 2026.

Soneto is a personal offline songbook app. It saves your song library on your device and lets you read saved songs offline.

Purchase help

If Pro access is missing after a purchase, use Restore Purchases in Soneto while signed in to the platform account used for the purchase. Manage or cancel a recurring subscription through your platform's subscription controls. Monthly and annual subscriptions renew according to the applicable store terms. A lifetime option is a one-time purchase.

Saved reading remains available without Pro. Pro is required for new imports.

Contact support

Soneto is provided by BFL Technologies Studio LTDA.

Email appstore@bfltechstudio.com. To help with a problem, include your app version, device model, iOS version, and the steps that reproduce the problem.

Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.
