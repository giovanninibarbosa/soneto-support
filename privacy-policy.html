Soneto Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026.

Soneto is a personal songbook app. This policy explains how the app handles your song library, imports, settings, purchases, and support information.

Soneto is provided by BFL Technologies Studio LTDA. You can contact us at appstore@bfltechstudio.com.

Your library and settings

Soneto stores your saved songs on your device. A saved song can include its title, artist, lyrics, chords, diagrams, source URL, musical key, selected reading key, and save/update dates. The app also stores preferences such as language, theme, font size, and scrolling speed on your device.

Text you paste, files you choose, and content you share into Soneto are used to prepare an import. Saving an import adds the song to your local library. Soneto does not provide an account, a cloud library, or app-operated library synchronization.

Requests to song sources

Searching for songs, discovering artists, and importing from CifraClub require internet access. The app sends search queries or requested page URLs to CifraClub's services. Like other internet services, these services can receive network information associated with the request, including your IP address. Their own policies govern their handling of those requests.

Soneto caches retrieved song pages on your device so it can reuse them. The app does not upload your saved library or pasted/file imports to CifraClub as part of these features.

Purchases and Pro access

Soneto uses your platform's app store and RevenueCat to process purchases, restore purchases, check Pro access, and provide purchase management. These services process purchase and entitlement information under their own policies. Soneto does not collect your payment-card details directly or ask you to create a Soneto account for a purchase.

Review RevenueCat's privacy policy, Apple's privacy policy, or Google's privacy policy for their handling of purchase, identifier, and device/network information.

Diagnostics and support

Soneto keeps a limited, temporary diagnostic buffer in app memory. It can include error details and a URL involved in a failed import. The app does not automatically send this buffer to a support or analytics server. When you choose to copy diagnostics, the information goes to your device's clipboard. Review it before sharing it with anyone.

If you email support, the recipient receives your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. Avoid sending payment details, passwords, or song content you do not have permission to share. The app does not include app-operated advertising or analytics.

Deletion, retention, and device services

Saved songs remain in the app's local library until you delete them or the app's data is removed. Deleting a song removes its library record; a cached copy of a previously retrieved web page may remain separately on the device. Preferences are also stored separately from songs.

Your operating system can manage backups, clipboard history, and shared content. Removing the app or its data does not necessarily remove an operating-system backup or information held by a purchase provider. Use your platform's controls for those services. RevenueCat and app-store retention are governed by their own policies.

Because Soneto does not provide cloud library synchronization, keep your device and any backups you choose to use secure. Device loss, app-data removal, or backup failure can result in loss of your local library.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or service providers change. The published policy will show its effective date.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Soneto support at appstore@bfltechstudio.com. See the Soneto support page.
